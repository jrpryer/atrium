::: page prose | The Charter
By the Crown at the top of Stairfall, in the two hundred and thirty-third year of the Stair Reckoning, under the seal of the falls, the tree, and four lamps:

Be it known that the courtyard at the foot of the city, called the Atrium, lying beneath the great tree and the falling water, shall not be left to the moss. Let there be a Keeper, and after that Keeper another, and another after that, who shall dwell in the lodge there and keep the stacks in order, the pools in health, the lamps in oil, and the stillness in good repair.

The Keeper shall want for nothing reasonable. [The original reads "nothing whatever". The copying clerk has taken a small liberty, in the interests of the treasury.] The Keeper shall be left in peace to do the work, and shall not be summoned up the stair for trifles.

Witnessed by three clerks of the Crown, one of whom had a cold.

::: page prose | Schedule the First, Of the Houses
Item, a library, to hold what the city writes and afterwards forgets it wrote. Its keepers shall shelve by sense and by sigil, and may argue about which.

Item, an apothecary, for remedies, tinctures, and the patient making of small useful things. [The original adds "and no explosions". This is thought to be a later hand.]

Item, the pools beneath the falls, to be tended by the Stillkeepers after the manner of their order, who shall read the water by its two breaths and keep the creatures in it content.

Item, a lodge for the Keeper, to be called the Sanctum, wherein the Keeper shall keep a journal of all that passes.

These four houses stand about the tree, and shall not be pulled down, sold, or let to cousins.

Copied fair from a fair copy, by a clerk of the Crown, who apologises for the blot in the margin and wishes it known that the blot was there before.
