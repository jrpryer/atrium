::: page prose | The Hollow
Past the flyleaf, the ledger stops being a ledger.

Its pages have been cut through, all together, with a small sharp knife, to make a hollow in the heart of the book. Whoever did it took their time. The cuts are straight, the corners square, and the shavings were swept away.

A thin border of each page survives around the hollow, still ruled into its old columns (date, depth, clarity) in a neat blue hand, with half a sum here and there running off the edge into nothing.

Tucked against one wall of the hollow is a scrap of note, folded once and then smoothed flat again:

"for safekeeping — not for the reliquary, not yet."
