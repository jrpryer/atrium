::: page prose | To the Apprentice
You came for gold and fire and colours in a glass. You get water, ash, and patience. Everyone does. I did.

Do not sulk. These three are the whole craft in small. Learn what they do to one another and you will know what most things do to most other things.

Begin with water. Water is honest; it shows you what you have done to it. Warm it and it grows restless and climbs, and will not come back down until it has cooled. Watch the lid, not the pot.

Then leave a bowl of water alone. I mean alone: no stirring, no peering, no breathing on it. Water made to wait long enough goes still in a way ordinary water never is, and still water is worth far more than it looks.

Patience, you will notice, is an ingredient. It is the one you will run out of first.

::: page recipe | Two Riddles of Ash
ASH, LEFT ALONE
  Take ash from a clean fire. Add nothing.
  Wait. Then wait more than that.
  What the fire left behind, given time,
  lies down and becomes earth again,
  dark and soft, the sort seeds like.

ASH, IN WATER
  Take ash. Take water. Put them together
  and they quarrel.
  What comes of the quarrel is a biting liquor.
  It bites fingers, it bites cloth, and it bites
  through the bottom of a cheap pot.
  Keep it in good glass. Do not taste it.
  You will want it later.

::: page prose | On Grinding, and on Explosions
The mortar is the most patient tool on the bench, and the most honest. Things that are ground give up their secrets: what they are made of, and what they were keeping to themselves. Seeds, stones, a thing you cannot name; grind a pinch and look. But only a pinch. What you grind does not come unground, not on this bench, and you may want the rest of it later.

As for explosions. I have had four. The first was the burner. The second was the burner. The third was the burner and some pride, and the fourth I will not discuss. None of them came from water, ash, or patience. Every one came from hurrying.

So: a small flame, clean glass, a pinch at a time. Write down everything, especially what does nothing. Nothing is an answer too. It is simply a shy one.
