# Atrium — World Bible (iteration 1)

> Working reference for content authors. Every proper noun here is a **placeholder** until the designer approves it
> (see README). Where this file and AGENTS.md disagree, AGENTS.md wins.

## Tone

Cozy, unhurried, warm, a little wry. Nothing is urgent. Loss is never permanent. People are kind and slightly
eccentric. Prose is concrete and sensory (smells, textures, light on water), never grimdark, never twee. Short
sentences are welcome. Humour is gentle and dry.

## The city: Stairfall

A city built down a cliff face in tiers, top to bottom:

| Tier | Character |
|---|---|
| **The High Hallows** | Not quite the city: mountain shrines *above* the Crown, cold springs, snow. Lantern Birches grow here and nowhere else. Pilgrims begin the **Long Stair** here. |
| **The Crown** | The top tier. Old authority, clerks, the charter office, the Crown's seal (*the falls, the tree, and four lamps*). Grand and a bit forgetful of the tiers below. |
| **The Gallery** | Markets, glassworks, gem-sellers, botanists with herbarium drawers, the Assayers' Exchange. The **Gallery Reach** is the waterworks at the Gallery's edge: three great sluices that feed the falls. |
| **The Ribs** | Cistern-quarters carved into the rock. Great dark cisterns. The **lamp-divers** dive them by sealed lamp to clear the intakes. Kitchen gardens on ledges. |
| **The Hem** | The lowest inhabited tier. Gardens, small trades, mourning houses. Red clay gets on everything ("everything in the Hem comes home red-footed"). |
| **The Atrium** | The canyon floor beneath everything. A forgotten mossy courtyard: the tree **Old Stoic**, turquoise pools, and four struggling houses. The player is its **Keeper**. |

## Landmarks

- **Old Stoic** — an enormous, ancient tree leaning out over the pools: big, old, serene and unchanging. It
  looks exactly as it always has and always will. Its bark is pocked with old square nail-holes at the height of a
  raised hand. It was the **last waystation** of the Long Stair.
- **The Slowsilver** — the waterfall from the high tiers. It once *ran silver*: when all three sluices at the
  Gallery Reach stood open, fine silver silt from the Hallows springs came down with the water and the falls
  glittered. Since **the Year of Quiet Water (SR 541)**, when **Sluice III** was closed "for repairs that never
  came", the falls are tall but lazy: a thin turquoise drift.
- **The pools** — fed by the falls, tended (once) by the **Stillkeepers**.

## The Charter

A centuries-old charter (SR 233) sets the Keeper's duties:

> *"Keep the stacks in order, the pools in health, the lamps in oil, and the stillness in good repair."*

## The four houses (locales)

- **The Library** — kept by the **Quire sisters, Ada and Mab** (elderly, bickering, devoted). Ada is precise
  (she wrote *The Geometry of Shelving*); Mab is a gossip and an amateur historian. Shelving rule of the house:
  *oldest nearest the lamp* (shelves are ordered by year, oldest first).
- **The Apothecary** — kept by **Tamsin Pell**, who inherited it from her master **Old Corran Pell**. Starts each
  apprentice on *water, ash, and patience*. Tools: Mortar & Pestle, Bunsen Burner, Weighing Scales, Magnifying
  Glass, the Discovery Map, and the *Old Formulary* (Corran's book of half-riddling recipes).
- **The Spirit Pools** — tended by the **Stillkeepers**, druids of stillness. They left the Atrium around SR 610
  when the pools clouded. **Brother Alder** comes back when the story begins to stir. Older Stillkeeper writer:
  **Sister Wend**.
- **The Sanctum** — the Keeper's own lodge: the journal desk, the Stillkeepers' **Balance** (an old riddle-lock
  set over a sealed reliquary), an assembly bench, and a chalk **Reconstitution Circle** in the floor that the
  Keeper takes for decoration at first.

## The two breaths of water (shared ontology)

Stillkeepers describe every pool by two breaths: **Light ↔ Dark** and **Hot ↔ Cold** (each measured -5 … +5).
Every creature has a place where it *survives* and a narrower place where it *thrives*. Neglected creatures never
die in a Stillkeeper's pool: they **sulk** (tuck into the reeds and refuse to breed) until things are put right.

Known lifeforms: Spotted Spinner, Blazing Glory, Sun-Spotted Goldfish, Yellow-backed Snail, Grey Guppy, Shadow
Guppy, Sun-Mote, Frost-Mote, Lantern Reed, Hush-Moss.

Breeding lore (true in the game rules):
- Like breeds like.
- *"Heat brings out the blaze"* — Spotted Spinners bred in very hot water sometimes hatch **Blazing Glories**.
- A **Spinner mother and a Glory father** make a **Sun-Spotted** child. *The other way round, they won't.*
- Grey Guppies bred in the **deepest shade** hatch **Shadow Guppies**.
- Motes are not bred; they **spawn** from minerals and auras laid in the spawning pond.

## Things and customs (lore threads the clue-objects rely on)

- **Votive sigil** — *a closed hand around a flame*: "the sign of things given to be kept" — offerings, tokens,
  vows. Shrines mark them. Libraries never shelve such things.
- **Vow-tokens** — Hallows pilgrims seal a **Lantern Birch leaf** in clear, hard **shrine-wax**, write a vow
  beneath the wax in brown ink, and wear it on a cord down the Long Stair. A token found means a vow still open.
- **Tithe-nails** — pilgrims beat a gold coin thin around a hand-forged iron nail and drive it into a waystation
  tree, "so the tree would hold the promise until they returned". Hallows-blessed gold glows faintly in water.
- **Smoke-pearls** — when a lamp-diver's sealed lamp gutters in the Ribs cisterns, a cistern-mussel may swallow the
  flame and wrap it in nacre for years: a black, seamless pearl, warm at its heart, lighter than it looks. Crack it
  and out comes smoke that will not disperse.
- **Jet** — black stone beads worn in the Hem for grief: drilled, strung on black cord, warm to the touch.
- **The lampstone** — the charter-lamps of the lower tiers were kindled from a single cold, pale, oval
  **lampstone**, carried down from the Crown each midsummer. It drank the sun at the top of the city and gave it
  back at the bottom. The Crown's charter-seal is cut into its base. Last carried down around SR 559; lost.
- **Gilded lamp-oil & the Lamplighter's Lantern** — pressed oil, a measure of patience, and *"something of true
  wealth, glittering and rare"*, warmed gently. Burned in a lamplighter's lantern it shows **lamplit ink**: the
  gold-ink wardens and pilgrims used for private notes, invisible by ordinary light.
- **Amber** — tree-resin turned stone: light as pumice, warms quickly in the hand, smells of resin near flame;
  sold in the Gallery; sometimes holds a sleeping insect.
- **Salamander eggs** — smooth, oval, cold as river stone until laid in sunlit water; snails gather on them.
- **The sluices** — three great sluices at the Gallery Reach; each opened by a heavy key with a **silver bit**
  (silver doesn't corrode in spring water), held by a **sluice-warden**. The warden of Sluice III in SR 541 is
  recorded only by initials: **I.F.**
- **The Stillkeepers' reliquaries** — kept "keys to things that should stay closed": small, warded keys.
- **The Balance** — *"Lay upon me a thing of shadow and of flame, and I will open."* Neither shadow alone nor fire
  alone will move it.
- **The Circle** — chalk circle in the Sanctum floor. *Matter is reclaimable; knowledge is permanent.* What is
  **made from** a thing goes quiet when the thing is made whole again; what it merely **touched** stays touched.
